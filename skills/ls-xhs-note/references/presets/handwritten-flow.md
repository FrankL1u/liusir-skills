# Chalkboard Style

Colorful chalk-on-board educational look with playful doodles and teaching energy.

## Best for

- tutorials
- step-by-step explainers
- informal classroom-style guidance

## Visual cues

- dark chalkboard background
- white main text with colored chalk accents
- arrows, checkmarks, circles, and doodles
- hand-drawn chalk texture

## Typography cues

- handwritten chalk lettering
- color hierarchy for key terms
- visible sketchy baseline and imperfect lines

## Do

- keep it instructional
- use arrows and step emphasis
- let the board texture stay visible

## Do not

- make shapes too geometric or digital
- add glossy UI aesthetics
- mix it with a dense realistic notebook look
