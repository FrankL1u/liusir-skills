# Study Notes Style

Dense handwritten study-sheet aesthetic with realistic note-taking energy.

## Best for

- study guides
- dense framework summaries
- tutorial recap
- case breakdowns

## Visual cues

- lined-paper or notebook feel
- blue / black handwriting
- red circles and notes
- yellow highlight emphasis

## Typography cues

- handwritten note style
- visible hierarchy through annotation, not polished layout
- compact blocks with margin notes

## Do

- keep the page information-dense
- show marks, circles, arrows, and highlights
- use it when the note really has enough content to fill the canvas

## Do not

- use it for sparse poster covers
- leave large empty areas
- make it look too clean or digital
