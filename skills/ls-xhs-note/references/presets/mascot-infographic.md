# Mascot Infographic Style

Friendly cartoon-infographic aesthetic with recurring mascots, clear teaching structure, and approachable information density.

## Best for

- friendly technical explainers
- recommendation guides
- community-facing overviews
- approachable educational notes

## Visual cues

- cute recurring characters or helper mascots
- warm illustrated objects and scene elements
- soft background with card-based modules
- icons and labels that feel playful but organized

## Typography cues

- rounded, readable display headings
- supportive body labels with high legibility
- friendly educational tone in text hierarchy

## Do

- use mascots to make structure easier to follow
- keep modules warm and inviting
- let illustrations support teaching, not distract from it

## Do not

- make it childish to the point of losing clarity
- overcrowd the page with too many characters
- use it for cold minimalist statement covers
