# Hand Doodle Style

Loose hand-drawn doodle aesthetic with playful contrast, casual sketch energy, and clear comparison framing.

## Best for

- versus pages
- concept clashes
- playful explainer covers
- lighter comparison notes

## Visual cues

- off-white paper background
- bold hand-drawn outlines
- small doodles, arrows, stars, and spark marks
- simple icons with a casual sketch feel

## Typography cues

- thick handwritten title
- playful labels and handwritten annotations
- uneven but readable letter rhythm

## Do

- keep the center conflict obvious
- let the page feel lively and informal
- use arrows and doodles to guide comparison

## Do not

- make it too polished or geometric
- overload it with dense academic copy
- turn it into a corporate infographic
