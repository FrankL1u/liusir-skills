# Notion Style

Minimalist hand-drawn line art with strong whitespace and clean hierarchy.

## Best for

- knowledge cards
- product or workflow summaries
- tool recommendations

## Visual cues

- white or off-white background
- black or dark-gray line work
- pastel accents only
- simple doodles and geometric marks

## Typography cues

- clean hand-drawn labels
- minimal decoration
- strong headline, restrained body copy

## Do

- keep structure clean
- let one information block breathe
- use light annotation instead of heavy ornament

## Do not

- overload with dense poster effects
- use dramatic gradients
- turn it into a lifestyle collage
