# Screen-Print Style

Bold poster art with limited colors, strong silhouettes, and editorial tension.

## Best for

- opinion pieces
- cultural commentary
- dramatic short conclusions
- strong opening slides

## Visual cues

- flat color blocks
- halftone or print-grain feel
- geometric framing
- symbolic, not literal, focal images

## Typography cues

- bold condensed lettering
- typography as part of composition
- high contrast over delicate detail

## Do

- keep one iconic focal idea
- limit the palette
- use negative space actively

## Do not

- fill it with dense tiny copy
- use soft gradients
- make it look like a photo or product mockup
